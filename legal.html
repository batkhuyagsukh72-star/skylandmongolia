Privacy policyНууцлалын бодлого

Azure Skyland Mongolia ("we") runs this website. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Questions: skylandmongolia@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you type into the trip request form: your name, your email or phone number, travel month, number of travellers, travel style, places and tour you are interested in, your message and the site language.
  • A one-way, scrambled code made from your internet address, kept only to stop spam (we cannot turn it back into your address).

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request, plan your trip and send you an offer.
  • To send you an automatic confirmation that we received your request, if you gave an email address.
  • To protect the form from spam and abuse.

We never sell your information and we do not send you marketing without asking first.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores trip requests in a secure database.
  • Resend delivers the emails about your request.
  • FormSubmit is used as a backup to deliver your request by email if our own system is unavailable.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • The 3D map loads map images from NASA GIBS and terrain data from Amazon Web Services; like any website you visit, they see your internet address when your browser fetches those images.

These services may store data outside Mongolia. Links to Instagram, Facebook and YouTube take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Stored on your device

The site remembers your language choice and the places you add to your trip, and keeps a copy of the tour list in your browser so it loads faster. This is not used to track you, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep trip requests for as long as we need them to plan and run your trip and to answer later questions about it, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your request sooner.

Your choices

Email skylandmongolia@gmail.com to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. We will reply within 30 days.

Booking termsЗахиалгын нөхцөл

Prices

Tour prices are per person per day in US dollars and depend on the size of your group. The website shows the total per person for the whole tour. Every itinerary lists what is included and what is not.

Booking and deposit

  • Send a trip request on this site or email us. We reply with a confirmed itinerary and price.
  • To confirm your reservation, a 10% deposit of the total tour cost is required. Your tour is officially confirmed once the deposit has been received.
  • We send payment details by email when we confirm your trip. We never publish bank details on the website; if you receive payment details from anywhere else, check with us first.
  • The remaining balance can be paid before the start of the tour or upon arrival in Mongolia.

Changes and cancellation

If you need to change dates or cancel, tell us in writing as early as possible. We agree the conditions for your booking, including what part of the deposit can be returned, in writing when we confirm it.

On the road

  • Itineraries can change because of weather, road conditions, closures or safety. Your guide and driver will choose the safest option and keep as close to the plan as possible.
  • Travel insurance that covers medical care and evacuation is strongly recommended.
  • You are responsible for your passport, visa and any health requirements for entering Mongolia.

Car rental

Car rental prices are per day. Fuel, car wash and the driver's fee (if you choose a driver) are paid separately. The rental conditions for your car are confirmed in writing before pick-up.
