    Why Skyland

    We believe the best way to discover Mongolia is not just by seeing it, but by living it — sharing tea with nomads, riding across the open steppe and sleeping under endless starry skies.

    Locally owned and operated

    Run by Mongolians, with Mongolian drivers and guides. Our founder travels and films the country for his vlog, so he knows these places first-hand.

    Authentic nomadic family experiences

    Nights with herder families along the route: share their tea, join the day’s work and sleep in their ger.

    Custom and private tours

    Private trips built around what you want to see, at your pace — no fixed bus tours, no rushing past the view.
